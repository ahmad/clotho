Clotho 1.0.7

  • Cleaner names. Tabs and workspaces show just their names. The branch and pull request stay in the strip at the bottom of the focused pane, and ⌘P still finds them.
  • ⌘/ hides the sidebar. Press it again to bring the sidebar back. The shortcuts overview moves to ⌥⌘/.
  • A bottom bar in the sidebar. Machines, Settings, and a light or dark switch. Right-click the switch to match macOS again.
  • Close a pane from its corner. Hover near a pane's top-right corner for a ×.
  • A friendlier empty workspace, with a New Tab button.
  • No more endless "Checking…". When a machine can't be reached, Clotho tries a few times, then shows a Reconnect button. It also tries again by itself when your Mac wakes or the network changes.
