Clotho 1.0.11

  • One Settings window. General and Machines now sit side by side as tabs. ⌘, opens General, and the sidebar's Machines button opens Machines.
  • A cleaner General tab. Notifications, Appearance (System, Light or Dark) and Updates, in tidy groups that fit without scrolling.
  • Machines at a glance. Each machine shows whether it's connected, plus its on/off switch. Add or remove machines with + and −, double-click one to edit it, and drag to reorder. The selected machine's own settings, such as scrollback and start at login, appear below the list.
  • Matching window buttons. The close, minimize and full-screen buttons are now the same size and in the same spot in every Clotho window.
