Clotho 1.0.12

  • ⌘P is a tree. It opens on this Mac's workspaces, with the current one open to its tabs. → opens a workspace, ← closes it, and ← again goes up to all your machines. ↑ and ↓ switch tabs as you go, and Esc takes you back to where you were.
  • Needs you. Agents waiting for you, or stuck on an error, on any machine, are listed at the top of ⌘P. Press Return to jump straight to one.
  • Search shows where things are. Type to search every machine as before. Each result now shows its machine and workspace. Tab opens the tree at that result, and clearing the search brings back the tree where you left it.
  • Quick keys on any row. ⌘⌫ closes a tab or workspace (after asking), ⌥↩ opens a new tab there, and ⌘1–⌘9 jump to a row. The keys are listed at the bottom of ⌘P.
  • Includes clothod 0.1.26.
