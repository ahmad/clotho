Clotho 1.0.1

  • Light mode for terminal apps. Apps that ask the terminal for its colours, such as Claude Code's Auto theme and vim, now see Clotho's light or dark background and follow it when you switch. In Claude Code, run /theme and pick Auto. Comes with clothod 0.1.20.
  • A simpler welcome window. One idea per page, and ← / → move between pages.
