Clotho 1.0.13

  • Commands in ⌘P. Type > to act on the selected row. Open a new tab or workspace there, split a pane, or start Claude or Codex in its folder (or resume your last Claude there). You can also rename, move or close a tab, open Files, Settings or Machines, switch appearance, turn a machine on or off, or reconnect.
  • Open a tab beside you. In ⌘P, press ⌘↩ on a tab to move it next to the pane you were in.
  • Move a tab to another workspace. Choose “Move Tab to…” and pick the workspace in the same list.
  • A sidebar button. Show or hide the sidebar from the button next to the window buttons, as well as with ⌘/.
  • Hide the Agents panel. Turn it off in Settings › General if you'd rather have more room for your workspaces. Agent dots, alerts and notifications still work.
  • The New Tab and New Workspace buttons now show their shortcuts.
