Clotho 1.0.18

  • Alerts say whether you're needed. A finished turn reads “Needs you” when the agent asks you something, and “Done” when it only finished, in notifications, the Agents panel and ⌘P.
  • A plain Done is silent. A finished turn with a question follows “Notify When Waiting for You”.
  • Alerts wait until an agent has really stopped, so a turn that carries on doesn't interrupt you.
  • Your phone is left alone while you're at the Mac.
  • Settings › Machines shows when a machine's “Summarize agent messages” isn't working, and why.
  • Needs clothod 0.1.31 on each machine; Clotho offers the update.
