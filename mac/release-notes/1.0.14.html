Clotho 1.0.14

  • Panes follow where you're looking. If you open a pane on your iPhone, it shrinks to fit the phone. Come back to it on the Mac (click into it, switch to its tab or bring Clotho to the front) and it fills the Mac's window again. Typing no longer changes a pane's size.
  • A pane's close button now stays in the pane's top-right corner when another device has made the pane smaller.
  • ⌘P commands match whole words: “>rename” finds Rename, not every command whose letters happen to appear in order.
